THE FOUNDING STORY

“I stood there for ten minutes. From the colours I could smell the hot and dry air. I walked out feeling like I had just come back from the South of France.”

— FOUNDER OF HEARTFUL

A few years ago, at the Courtauld Institute of Art in London, our founder stood before Monet’s Antibes (1888). In the middle of a hard winter, the painting transported her entirely. She realised that this power — to heal, to nourish, to move — had only been available to her because of years of art training. Heartful exists to unlock that for everyone.
